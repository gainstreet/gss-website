A world on paper.

Every game starts somewhere. Ours started with a comic book Jack made.

His characters, stories and imagination became Pumpkin Sooper. Together, Jack and his dad, Tom, turned those ideas into a game you can actually play.

It’s a father-and-son project from Glenmore, Kelowna. And now, it’s a way to show up for a family in our community.

THIS ONE’S FOR CLYDE. ♥

A LITTLE GAME. A LOCAL CAUSE.

Play for Clyde.

Clyde is a young boy in our Glenmore neighbourhood who has been diagnosed with MLD (metachromatic leukodystrophy). His family are friends of ours, and we wanted to find a way to help.

All profits from Pumpkin Sooper, after costs, will go to Clyde and his family.

You can help by buying the game, donating directly to the family’s GoFundMe, or sharing their story.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before you fly.

Where can I play?

Pumpkin Sooper is available now on the Apple App Store for iPhone and iPad. The game is played in landscape orientation. The Android version is in preparation and is not available on Google Play yet.

How does buying the game help?

Jack and Tom have committed to giving all profits from Pumpkin Sooper, after costs, to Clyde and his family. This means the amount remaining after costs, rather than the entire purchase price. To make a direct donation instead, use the family’s GoFundMe.

Are there ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases?

No. Pumpkin Sooper is a one-time purchase with no ads, subscriptions or real-money in-app purchases. Coins earned while playing can be used in the game. The Canadian App Store price is CA$5.99; local prices may vary.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No player account is required. Gameplay works offline and progress is saved on your device. Store downloads and external links require an internet connection.

Where do I find support or age-rating information?

See the App Store listing for compatibility and age-rating information. For help, visit game support or read the privacy policy.
